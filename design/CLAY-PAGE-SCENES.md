# Original animated clay page scenes

Created 27 November 2026 with the built-in image generation tool. These are fictional educational illustrations, not FCC staff or customer photographs.

Eight subject-family scenes are paired with the existing distinct topic illustration for each route. The home hero is untouched. Website files use alpha-preserving WebP exports at 900 and 480 pixels; source generation PNGs are retained outside the repository. Animation is CSS-based; motion pauses off-screen, in an inactive tab, at the global pause control and under reduced-motion preferences.

Saved website assets: `dist/assets/scenes/academic-clay-v1.webp` and `dist/assets/scenes/academic-clay-v1-480.webp`.

Prompt: Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original inner-page hero illustration for Future Cloud Campus, an online education website. Create ONE polished sculptural 3D clay illustration: two school learners at a small sculptural desk, working together on a notebook beside a cobalt laptop, number blocks and a geometric pyramid, supportive online tutor visible on the screen. Cobalt blue, warm cream, sage mint and touches of butter yellow, matching an existing clay educational website.
