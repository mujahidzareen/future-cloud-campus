Build the foundations for Quran reading, starting with letter recognition and sounds and moving towards joined letters and short combinations.

THE LEARNING APPROACH

Assessment, practice and review.

01

Initial placement

Discuss age, prior reading and preferred times. The teacher can check letter recognition or listen to a short reading sample.

02

Teacher match

Ask admissions about teacher fit, the selection process and gender matching availability for your schedule.

03

Progress feedback

Review the previous lesson, fluency and Tajweed. The evaluation can record strengths, practice areas and the recommended pace.
