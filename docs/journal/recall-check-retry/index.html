A short recall, check and retry routine

Try a compact routine that ends with something you can verify.

01

Recall without the notes

Choose a small topic and write what you can explain from memory. Draw a diagram, list the steps of a method or answer a short question. Leave space where you are unsure.

02

Check the gap

Compare with your class notes or a trusted worked example. Use another colour to add what you missed. Look for one specific gap, such as a definition, a formula choice or a missing step.

03

Retry with a fresh question

Close the notes and try a related question. Explain why each step is needed. If you still need the example, make the task smaller and try again.

04

Return later

Keep the question or prompt so you can revisit it in another session. Your next attempt gives you something concrete to compare with the first.
