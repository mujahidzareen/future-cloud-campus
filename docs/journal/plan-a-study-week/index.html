STUDY PLANNING

A study week with room to breathe

Turn a list of topics into a few realistic next steps.

01

Choose one outcome

Before adding sessions to a calendar, decide what you want to be able to do. 'Complete three simultaneous-equation questions without looking at the worked example' gives a session a clearer purpose than 'do maths'. Keep the outcome small enough to check.

02

Work around the week you have

Mark school, travel, activities and rest first. Put the most demanding task in a time when you can concentrate. A shorter session with a clear goal can be easier to finish than a long block with no defined task.

03

Close the loop

At the end, answer one question without the notes. Record what you managed and the step that still needs attention. Use that information to choose the next task, rather than repeating the same topic from the beginning.

04

Leave some flexibility

Keep a small unallocated slot for work that takes longer than expected. If a task is repeatedly postponed, reduce its size or ask for help identifying the starting point.
