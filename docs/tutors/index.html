WHAT MAKES A GOOD MATCH?

More than the subject name.

Use the first conversation to make the practical details and learning approach clear.

◎

Course fit

Share the subject, school year, curriculum and exam board. Ask about the tutor’s relevant experience and qualifications.

✺

Learning fit

Bring a recent question. Discuss pace, explanations, independent practice and how feedback will be shared.

◷

Practical fit

Confirm the teaching language, local lesson time, session length, platform, total fee and cancellation arrangements.
