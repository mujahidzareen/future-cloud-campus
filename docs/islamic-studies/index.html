LESSON 01 OF 06

Belief and sincerity

ایمان اور اخلاص

Begin by learning about Allah’s oneness and the meaning of worship. Read Surah Al-Ikhlas with its Urdu translation, then discuss its message with your teacher.

YOUR PRACTICE

Try it with your teacher.

Read the complete short surah. In your own words, explain one thing you learned without replacing the published translation.

Read Quran 112:1 with Urdu translation
LESSON 02 OF 06

Cleanliness and wudu

پاکیزگی اور وضو

Learn why cleanliness matters before prayer. Read the Quranic passage about purification, then ask your teacher to demonstrate the steps of wudu and explain the practice followed in your family.

YOUR PRACTICE

Try it with your teacher.

Describe the steps you observed. Ask about anything you are unsure of, including what to do when water cannot be used.

Read Quran 5:6 with Urdu translation
LESSON 03 OF 06

Salah and a daily routine

نماز اور روزمرہ معمول

Learn the names of the five daily prayers, prepare a calm place and ask your teacher to demonstrate the movements and recitations. Build understanding alongside a consistent routine.

FajrDawn
DhuhrAfter midday
AsrAfternoon
MaghribAfter sunset
IshaNight
YOUR PRACTICE

Try it with your teacher.

Name the five prayers in order. Use a reliable local timetable with your family; prayer times depend on location and date.

Read Quran 2:43 with Urdu translation
LESSON 04 OF 06

Quranic duas

قرآنی دعائیں

Read a supplication for knowledge and a supplication for good in this life and the next directly from the Quran reader. Keep the Arabic and the published Urdu meaning together.

YOUR PRACTICE

Try it with your teacher.

Open Surah Ta-Ha 20:114. Also read Al-Baqarah 2:201 using the surah and ayah controls. Ask your teacher to help with pronunciation.

Read Quran 20:114 with Urdu translation
LESSON 05 OF 06

Kindness and respect

حسن اخلاق

Practise respectful speech, avoid hurtful labels and listen before replying. Read the Quranic reference, then discuss an everyday situation where your words can help someone.

YOUR PRACTICE

Try it with your teacher.

Describe a kind response to a classmate who makes a mistake. Choose one practical action for this week.

Read Quran 49:11 with Urdu translation
LESSON 06 OF 06

Parents and gratitude

والدین اور شکرگزاری

Learn to express gratitude to Allah and appreciation for parents. Read the referenced ayah with its translation and discuss age-appropriate ways to show care at home.

YOUR PRACTICE

Try it with your teacher.

Choose one helpful task and one way to thank a parent or caregiver. Discuss the passage with your teacher.

Read Quran 31:14 with Urdu translation
